DIY MAKER GUIDE

Cardboard ESP board holder

A practical, low-cost holder made from 3 mm cardboard. The English guide shows the 3.4 × 9.7 cm board opening, adjustable hand openings and step-by-step assembly.

Tap the guide to view it at full resolution. This is an illustrated assembly guide, not a 1:1 printable cutting stencil. Measure the opening before cutting.

Core components

01 · THE BOARD

Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch LCD 3.49B

640 × 172 IPS capacitive touchscreen, onboard QMI8658 6-axis sensor, ESP32-S3 and microSD functionality.

Waveshare product page ↗

02 · SHOT CONTROL

Built-in touchscreen

Tap the ESP32 screen to fire. The capacitive touch panel provides comfortable and direct shot control.

03 · THE STOCK

Wood, cardboard or 3D print

A stable, comfortable holder for repeatable movement. The first working prototype uses a simple DIY approach.

Safety: This is a non-firing electronic simulator. Use an appropriate dedicated training stock. Never work on or attach electronics to a loaded firearm.

What is still being prepared?
