BUILD

Make your own
electronic trainer.

The original concept uses a compact sensor-equipped display, a trigger button and a simple stock. No special computer is needed during normal use.

Core components

01 · THE BOARD

Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch LCD 3.49B

640 × 172 IPS capacitive touchscreen, onboard QMI8658 6-axis sensor, ESP32-S3 and microSD functionality.

Waveshare product page ↗

02 · THE TRIGGER

12 mm momentary button

A spring-return push button mounted where the index finger naturally rests. Wiring varies with the build.

03 · THE STOCK

Wood, cardboard or 3D print

A stable, comfortable holder for repeatable movement. The first working prototype uses a simple DIY approach.
