THE GOODWILL CHAIN

You don't have to buy.
You can just play.

Try a device. If you like it, you can buy a ready-to-play one for a friend, hand over your own, or just tell someone about the game. All three are welcome, and none is required. If buying a new device, arrange payment and shipping privately with the seller.

01 · CHALLENGE

Invite a person

Send a friendly challenge through email or Facebook. They can say yes, no, or simply try it.

02 · SHARE (OPTIONAL)

New or passed on

Buy a ready-to-play board for a friend, pass on your own, or invite them to get one themselves. No purchase is required.

03 · GROW

Leave a trace, if you wish

Tell us who challenged whom so the forest can grow. Private handovers we never hear about stay outside the public tree.

Start with a message.

Copy an invitation and send it using your own email or Facebook account. No public address forms.
